## 激活安装Typora_v1.9.5

#### 1.首先下载1.9.5版本（1.9.5以下也可以）以及激活工具

[Typora_v1.9.5下载链接](https://pan.quark.cn/s/349d39063149)

[Typora_v1.9.5激活工具下载链接](https://pan.quark.cn/s/20fb6f8605b3)

#### 2.进入Typora安装文件夹中，将解压后的两个激活文件复制进去

![image-20251231082154127](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/dagongdeyatou/img-bed@img/blog/202512/20251231082155242_2025-12-31_084453.png)

#### 3.在Typora安装文件夹中打开命令行cmd，分别输入如下命令运行激活工具

```cmd
node_inject.exe
```

![image-20251231081820783](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/dagongdeyatou/img-bed@img/blog/202512/20251231081822160_2025-12-31_084500.png)

```cmd
license-gen.exe
```

![image-20251231081953305](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/dagongdeyatou/img-bed@img/blog/202512/20251231081954469_2025-12-31_084505.png)

#### 4.运行Typora，输入上一步获取到的激活码即可激活成功

![image-20251231081932679](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/dagongdeyatou/img-bed@img/blog/202512/20251231081933881_2025-12-31_084511.png)
